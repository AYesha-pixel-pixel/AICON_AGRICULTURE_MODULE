This registry records the agricultural sources considered for the guidance
knowledge base. A URL being listed here does not mean that every claim in a
guidance entry has been verified. Source content was inspected only where
access was available on 10 October 2026.

## Provenance status meanings

- `general_reference_available`: relevant general source content has been
  inspected, but local applicability is not established.
- `local_verification_required`: the source is inaccessible, incomplete for
  the claim, or not sufficient to establish advice for the relevant crop and
  region.
- `verified_for_local_use`: reserved for a claim supported by an inspected,
  authoritative source applicable to the relevant Pakistani crop and region.
  No guidance entry currently has this status.

## Current review boundary

The six model disease labels are not diagnoses. Guidance remains blocked unless
the decision rules approve the prediction. Disease entries retain explicit
source-verification warnings, and no pesticide product, dose, rate, or
treatment schedule is included.
